03 / LEADERSHIP

Experience that
informs our approach.

The business draws on Fernel Campher's background in senior humanitarian leadership, programme delivery and institutional development.

His professional experience includes serving as National Programmes Manager, Acting CEO / Acting Secretary General and Western Cape Provincial Manager within the South African Red Cross Society. His published operational references include IFRC disaster response reporting and international first-aid and climate-adaptation initiatives.

These roles and published projects describe the founder's prior professional work. They are not presented as contracts performed by Campher & B-Yond.
